Booking

How do we book?
Each villa books directly on its own listing. The buttons on the villa pages go straight there, and the platform handles payment, dates, confirmation and current pricing. Casa Suerte South books on Airbnb; Casa Suerte North books on Vrbo.
Booking both houses works differently: send one inquiry through the site and we hold both calendars together, so you are never juggling two reservations and hoping they line up.
Can we book both villas together?
Yes, that is the point of the pair. The two houses share a property line, so booking both gives one group 10 bedrooms, 11 bathrooms and beds for 20, with two kitchens and two pools.
Use the inquiry form rather than booking each house separately: one conversation, both calendars held, no risk of winning one house and losing the other.
What is the minimum stay?
The standard minimum is 4 nights. Holiday weeks and peak season may carry a longer minimum, so ask when you inquire and we will tell you exactly what applies to your dates.
What is the cancellation policy?
Cancellation terms are set out in full in the rental agreement you receive before you pay a deposit, and we will summarize them in plain language in your first reply.
Bookings made through Airbnb or Vrbo follow the cancellation policy shown on that listing.
How quickly do you reply?
Every inquiry is answered personally within 24 hours.
Inquiries go straight to the owners, not to an agency queue.

The villas

How close together are the two villas?
They are immediate neighbors, sharing a property line and the same stretch of beach. You can walk between them in well under a minute without leaving the properties.
Each house keeps its own entrance, kitchen, pool and terrace, so two families can share the beach and still have separate front doors.
If we book one villa, who is next door?
When you book a single villa, the other house may be occupied by a separate party. Tell us at the inquiry stage if privacy across both properties matters to you and we will talk through the dates where booking both makes sense.
Do both villas have air conditioning?
Both houses are set up for the Baja climate with air conditioning and ceiling fans. We will confirm the exact coverage room by room when you inquire.
Is there internet good enough to work from?
Cell coverage across the East Cape is patchy, so the villas do not rely on it. We will confirm the current connection and typical speeds when you inquire. Guests have run full remote work weeks from the terrace.
Are the pools heated?
Heating can be added for an extra nightly charge, which is worth having in the cooler months. Tell us your dates when you inquire and we will confirm the current rate for the house you are booking.

Getting here

How far is the airport?
Los Cabos International (SJD) is about 60 minutes away, on paved highway for effectively the whole drive.
Most guests pick up a rental car at the airport. Having a vehicle matters here, because this is not a walk-to-dinner location.
Do we need a 4x4?
A standard car handles the highway and the community. If you plan to chase surf breaks or take the back roads toward the Pacific side, a high-clearance vehicle or 4x4 is worth the upgrade. We send road notes for the final approach with your pre-arrival information.
Where should we buy groceries?
The easiest option is to have the shopping done for you. Prickly Pear at pricklypearcabo.com is a Los Cabos grocery delivery service, and ordering ahead means the kitchen is stocked when you walk in rather than after a supermarket run. Confirm delivery to the East Cape when you place the order.
If you prefer to shop yourself, stock up in San José del Cabo on the drive out. The large supermarkets there have the widest selection you will see all week.
Closer in, La Ribera is about 20 minutes away for smaller grocery stores, restaurants and fuel, and El Mercadito at Costa Palmas is good for provisions and specialty items.
What time is check-in and check-out?
Check-in from 3:00 PM, check-out by 11:00 AM. If your flight lands awkwardly, ask. We flex where the calendar allows.

The area

Is the beach actually swimmable?
Yes, and it is the reason to be on this coast rather than the Corridor or the Pacific side, where most beaches are not safe for swimming.
The water in front of the villas is calm enough to swim, paddle and snorkel directly off the sand. Conditions vary with the weather, as they do anywhere, so read the water on the day.
How far is Cabo Pulmo?
About 25 minutes, roughly 15 miles south. It is the only hard-coral reef in the Sea of Cortez and the site of the largest fish-biomass recovery ever measured in a marine reserve.
You snorkel and dive it with permitted local guides from the village, and the village runs largely on cash, so bring pesos.
Can we eat at Costa Palmas or the Four Seasons?
Costa Palmas is about 20 minutes away and several of its restaurants take outside reservations, though access policies change by season and venue.
Call ahead to confirm and book, because in high season the good tables go early. Note that the villas are independently owned and are not affiliated with Four Seasons or Costa Palmas.
Is it safe?
Lighthouse Point Estates is a gated community with 24-hour guarded entry, and the East Cape generally is a quiet stretch of coast.
Take the same precautions you would anywhere: lock the car, do not leave valuables on the beach, and drive the highway in daylight where you can. Livestock on the road after dark is the real hazard here.

Practical

Should we bring cash?
Yes. Cabo Pulmo dive operators, small restaurants and many local services are cash-only, and the nearest reliable ATMs are not next door.
Pesos go further than dollars almost everywhere outside the resorts.
Is the water safe to drink?
Each house has a water cooler with purified water supplied, so drinking water is taken care of from the moment you arrive. Use it rather than the tap, as you would anywhere in Mexico.
Do you allow events or weddings?
Booking both villas puts a full group on one property line, which suits small weddings, reunions and retreats. Event use needs to be agreed in advance and is subject to community rules, so tell us what you have in mind when you inquire.
Are pets allowed?
Not at present. If that changes we will say so here first.
