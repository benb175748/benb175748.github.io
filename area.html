The area

Guides to the quiet end of Baja

Written from the villas out. Every drive time is from our gate, and every recommendation is one we'd give across the kitchen counter.

  • 25 minutes from the villas

    Cabo Pulmo National Marine Park: a visitor guide

    The only hard-coral reef in the Sea of Cortez, 25 minutes down the coast, and the site of the largest fish recovery ever measured in a marine reserve.

  • 20 minutes from the villas

    Costa Palmas and Four Seasons Los Cabos: a guide for East Cape guests

    Milos, Mozza, a 12-seat tasting room, a Robert Trent Jones II course and a deep-water marina, all a 20-minute drive up the coast. Here is how to use Costa Palmas without staying there.

  • From the beach out front

    Sportfishing the East Cape

    Striped, blue and black marlin offshore, roosterfish in the shorebreak out front, and a marina twenty minutes up the coast. How to fish the East Cape, season by season.

  • Often visible from the beach

    Whale season on the East Cape

    Humpbacks and gray whales move through the Sea of Cortez from mid-December to mid-April, and the show often reaches the beach in front of the villas.

  • 60 minutes from SJD airport

    Best time to visit the East Cape, and how to get here

    November through May is the prime window on the East Cape. Here is what each season does to the weather and the water, plus how the airport arrival and drive work.

  • 20 to 60 minutes by 4x4

    East Cape surf: a day-trip guide to the breaks

    Point breaks strung along a dirt coast road north and south of the villas, best from July to September, and reachable in a morning if you leave early.

  • 35 minutes from the villas

    Los Barriles: wind, water and the East Cape kitesurfing season

    The East Cape wind town, 35 minutes north: reliable November-to-March northerlies, kite and windsurf conditions, beach horseback riding and desert ATV routes.

  • 55 minutes from the villas

    San Jose del Cabo: the Gallery District, the plaza and the Art Walk

    The colonial town at the other end of the East Cape road, with a working gallery district, a Thursday evening Art Walk from November to June, and the last serious supermarkets before the villas.
