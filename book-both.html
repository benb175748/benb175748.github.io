The layout

Close enough to share, far enough to breathe

The houses sit side by side above the same beach, each with its own pool, palapa and parking. No shared walls, no shared kitchen queue, just a short barefoot walk between the two.

Who books the pair

Built for groups that don't want to share one roof

  • Multi-family trips

    Each family gets a front door, a kitchen, and a pool. The kids run between houses; the adults get their evenings back. Shared beach, separate bedtimes.

  • Reunions & milestone birthdays

    Beds for 20 without an air mattress in sight. Cook together in one house, escape to the other, and put three generations on one stretch of sand.

  • Weddings & celebrations

    House the wedding party next to the couple, not across town. Small ceremonies on the property are possible by advance arrangement, subject to community rules.

  • Corporate retreats

    Two houses split naturally into work and living space. Sessions run under one palapa, meals under the other, and a swimmable beach sits where the breakout room should be.

  • Fishing weeks

    Roosterfish from the beach out front, blue water twenty minutes away at Costa Palmas Marina, and two full kitchens ready for whatever comes back on ice.

  • Remote-work months

    Split the pair between two households and trade office hours for shore hours. Longer stays are welcome, so ask about monthly rates in the shoulder seasons.

Group-stay questions

The practical bits

Can we book both villas together?
Yes, that is the point of the pair. The two houses share a property line, so booking both gives one group 10 bedrooms, 11 bathrooms and beds for 20, with two kitchens and two pools.
Use the inquiry form rather than booking each house separately: one conversation, both calendars held, no risk of winning one house and losing the other.
What is the minimum stay?
The standard minimum is 4 nights. Holiday weeks and peak season may carry a longer minimum, so ask when you inquire and we will tell you exactly what applies to your dates.
How close together are the two villas?
They are immediate neighbors, sharing a property line and the same stretch of beach. You can walk between them in well under a minute without leaving the properties.
Each house keeps its own entrance, kitchen, pool and terrace, so two families can share the beach and still have separate front doors.
Do you allow events or weddings?
Booking both villas puts a full group on one property line, which suits small weddings, reunions and retreats. Event use needs to be agreed in advance and is subject to community rules, so tell us what you have in mind when you inquire.
